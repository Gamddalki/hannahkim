> Program Note

There is no universal “now.” As physicist Carlo Rovelli reminds us, **time exists only in relation to something else** — which means simultaneity is closer to an illusion than a fact. Moving along our separate trajectories, we often forget this. Yet, we were here together, in this space, at some moment. That fact does not change.

_PresentS_ (2026) is a generative audiovisual work that traces this paradox through cosmic stardust. Driven by a real two-channel stereo dialogue, two particle systems unfold across a vast canvas, each with its own light and gravity. As the dialogue deepens, the two systems draw closer. The gravity we gave each other leaves a mark. The bent trajectories stay with us, even after the moment has passed.

_— With the voices of Jaeseo Kim and the artist_
